Bonus · Rainy Day Play Plan · for dogs

No walk today?
Here's the plan.

12 indoor games that use things you already have, and a simple week to rotate them, so the projector and the toys stay new.

How to use this

  • Pick two games a day. Ten to twenty minutes total is plenty for most dogs.
  • Mix one "nose" or "brain" game with one "move" game. Sniffing and thinking tire a lot of dogs out as much as running.
  • Stop while he's still having fun, and always finish with something calm.

The 12 games

Nose · 5 min
1. Find It

Show him a treat, let him watch you put it under one of three cups, then say "find it". When he gets it every time, hide treats around one room while he waits in another.

Nose · 3 min
2. The Towel Burrito

Roll a few small treats inside an old towel, loosely. He noses and paws it open. Supervise so the towel doesn't become a chew toy.

Nose · 5 min
3. Muffin Tin Puzzle

Treats in a few cups of a muffin tin, tennis balls on top of every cup. He has to move the balls to find which ones pay off.

Nose · 5 min
4. Box Shred

An empty cardboard box with crumpled paper and a few treats inside. Some dogs love tearing it apart. Pick up the pieces after, and skip it if he swallows cardboard.

Move · 5 min
5. Hallway Fetch

A soft toy, a long hallway, closed doors on the sides. Short throws, slow rolls for older dogs.

Move · 3 min
6. Stair Sprints

Only for healthy adult dogs who are steady on stairs. Toss a toy up a few steps, call him back down slowly. Skip it for puppies, seniors and long-backed breeds.

Move · 5 min
7. Tug With Rules

He grabs on "take it", lets go on "drop it" for a treat, then you start again. The rules are the game.

Bond · 5 min
8. Hide and Seek

Someone holds him (or ask for a sit-stay), you hide behind a door, then call once. Easy spots first.

Brain · 5 min
9. Name That Toy

Pick one toy, say its name each time he touches it, reward. After a few days, put it next to another toy and ask for it by name.

Brain · 2 min
10. Two-Minute Tricks

Spin, paw, touch your hand with his nose. Short and fun, and stop while he still wants more.

The kit · 10 min
11. Lights and Catch

Dim the lights, run the projector for 5 to 8 minutes, then switch it off and drop the squeaky squirrel. See the First Hunt Guide.

Rest · 15 min
12. The Calm-Down Chew

End every rainy-day session with something to chew in his bed: a safe chew or a stuffed food toy. It tells him play time is over.

A rainy week

MondayFind It + Two-Minute Tricks
TuesdayLights and Catch + Calm-Down Chew
WednesdayMuffin Tin Puzzle + Hallway Fetch
ThursdayTug With Rules + Name That Toy
FridayLights and Catch + Calm-Down Chew
Weekend rainHide and Seek + Box Shred + Calm-Down Chew

Rotating keeps things new. Most dogs lose interest in a toy they see every day, so put the projector and the squirrel away between sessions.

Safety

  • Clear the floor of anything he could slip on or knock over.
  • Use small, soft treats and count them into his daily food.
  • Skip running and stair games for puppies, seniors, and dogs with joint or back problems.
  • Indoor games add to walks and outdoor play; they don't replace them.
