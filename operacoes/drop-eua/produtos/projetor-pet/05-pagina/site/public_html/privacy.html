Privacy policy

This policy explains what (operated by ) collects when you visit or buy, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

When you buy: your name, email, shipping address and order details. Card details go straight to Stripe, our payment processor; we never see or store your full card number.

When you visit: pages viewed, clicks, device type, and how you arrived (for example, an ad link). We use a first-party cookie to recognize your visit.

Who we share it with

  • Stripe, to process payments and prevent fraud.
  • Our fulfillment partner, to pack and ship your order (name and address only).
  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram), through the Meta Pixel and Conversions API, to measure our ads. Emails and addresses sent to Meta are hashed first.
  • Microsoft Clarity, to see anonymized heatmaps of how people use the page.

We don't sell your personal information for money.

Your choices

Click "Opt out" on the cookie banner, or opt out here, to stop ad measurement cookies on this browser. You can also email us to see, correct or delete your data.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

You have the right to know what personal information we collect and how it's used, to ask us to delete or correct it, and to opt out of the "sharing" of your information for cross-context behavioral advertising (our use of the Meta Pixel counts as sharing under California law). Use the opt-out link above or email . We won't treat you differently for using these rights. We honor Global Privacy Control signals as an opt-out.

How long we keep it

Order records for as long as tax and accounting rules require. Visit data for up to 13 months.

Kids

This site isn't directed to children under 13.
