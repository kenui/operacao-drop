Privacy policy

This policy explains what (operated by ) collects when you visit or buy, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

When you buy: your name, email, shipping address and order details. Card details go straight to Stripe, our payment processor; we never see or store your full card number.

When you visit: pages viewed, clicks, device type, and how you arrived (for example, an ad link). We use first-party cookies to recognize your visit and to connect a purchase to the ad that brought you. Your phone number is used for delivery updates about your order and, hashed, for ad measurement.

Who we share it with

  • Stripe, to process payments and prevent fraud.
  • Our fulfillment partner, to pack and ship your order (name and address only).
  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram), through the Meta Pixel and Conversions API, to measure our ads. Emails, phone numbers, names and addresses sent to Meta are hashed first.
  • Microsoft Clarity, to see anonymized heatmaps of how people use the page.

Your Privacy Choices

We don't sell your personal information for money. We do share some of it with Meta to measure and improve our ads: pages you view, items you add, and, when you check out, your email, phone, name and city/state/ZIP. These are hashed (scrambled) before they leave our server or your browser. Under California and some other state laws, this counts as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising.

When this box is on, or when your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we tell Meta to process your data under its Limited Data Use mode, and we turn off Microsoft Clarity. You can also email to see, correct or delete your data. We won't treat you differently for using these rights.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

You have the right to know what personal information we collect and how it's used, to ask us to delete or correct it, and to opt out of the "sharing" of your information for cross-context behavioral advertising. Use the box above or email . We honor Global Privacy Control signals as an opt-out.

How long we keep it

Order records for as long as tax and accounting rules require. Visit data for up to 13 months.

Kids

This site isn't directed to children under 13.
