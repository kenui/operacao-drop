Returns & refunds

You have 30 days from delivery to ask for a refund, for any reason, including "my pet just stared at it."

How to ask

Email with your order number. That's it. You don't need to fill out a form.

Do I need to send it back?

Usually not. If we do ask you to return it, we'll send a prepaid label, so it costs you nothing.

When the money comes back

We refund to the card you paid with within 2 business days of your email. Banks usually show it in 5 to 10 business days.

Add-ons

The spare plush pack, a second kit and the PDF guide are covered the same way. You can refund any item on its own.

Please email us first

If something's wrong, write to us before opening a dispute with your bank. A refund from us is faster than a bank dispute.
