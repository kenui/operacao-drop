```
One continuous first-person take filmed on a smartphone by a woman sitting on her couch. She holds the phone at chest height with her left hand and does everything else with her right hand. No cuts, no zoom, no transitions. Natural hand shake and small reframes, like a real person filming with one hand.

AVATAR LOCK: we only ever see her hands, forearms and legs. Fair skin, short bare nails, oversized cream crewneck sweatshirt cuffs with light pilling, black leggings. Her face never appears.

ANIMAL LOCK: one small adult French bulldog, compact and muscular, short smooth blue-gray coat, white narrow blaze on the chest, large upright bat ears with pink inner skin, dark brown eyes, short black muzzle, wet nose, thin red nylon collar. Same dog the whole take. His mouth stays closed and empty: he never bites, licks or carries anything.

PRODUCT LOCK: a rigid cream-white plastic paw, about 17 cm tall, with four black toe pads and one large black center pad, fixed upright on a round matte black base. It never bends, grows, shrinks or moves by itself. It is OFF until she presses the button: until that moment there is no light on it and no light shape anywhere on the floor.

ROOM LOCK: same dim living room the whole take. The floor lamp stays on. Blue dusk in the window. Nobody touches a light switch.

0.0-2.5 s: Her right hand pulls the clear plastic bag off the paw device and lifts the device out of the cardboard box on her lap. The dog, standing beside her on the couch, pushes his nose after it, sniffing fast, ears forward. She moves it a little away from his nose.
2.5-4.5 s: She leans forward; the phone tips down toward the bare hardwood floor right in front of the couch. Her right hand stands the device upright on the floor, about one meter ahead. The dog hops off the couch and sniffs the black base.
4.5-5.5 s: Her index finger presses the button on the black base. One clear plastic click. A small blue light turns on on the base.
5.5-8.0 s: She sits back; the phone rises to show the floor between the device and the far wall. Only now, five flat rings of orange light lie on the wood floor in front of the device, like a projector image, slightly dimmer at the edges, following the wood grain. LOOK: unedited phone video, natural sensor noise in the shadows, slight motion blur on fast moves, real fabric and fur texture, no filter, no on-screen text, no logos.
```
